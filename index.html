FAQ

Questions people actually ask

What platforms do you manage?

Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, and X, plus paid campaigns on Meta, TikTok, and LinkedIn Ads. We'll recommend which ones are actually worth your time rather than managing all of them by default.

Do you work with individuals, or only companies?

Both. We run full brand accounts for companies and personal-brand accounts for founders, executives, and creators — often for the same client at once.

How long is the contract?

Month to month. There's no long-term lock-in — we'd rather earn the renewal every month than hold you to a term.

Who creates the content — us or you?

We handle strategy, writing, editing, and design in-house. If you have existing footage, brand assets, or a strong point of view, we build around it — otherwise we can produce from scratch.

How fast can we start?

Most accounts are live within a week of the initial audit and strategy call.

How much does this cost?

Depends on scope — platforms, content volume, and whether paid social is included. Send us a message and we'll put a proposal together.
